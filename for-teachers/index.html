Which books to start with

  • New to letter sounds: the Stage 1 to 3 readers, starting with Pip Sits.
  • Moving on to sh, ch, blends and vowel teams: the eight Reading Trail stories, from Pocketful of Sounds to Panda-Cake’s Puddle Party.
  • Confident early readers: the four-page short reads, each built around one friendship, kindness or adventure idea.
  • Maths lessons: the number stories on the bookshelf.

Ways to use them in class

  • Computer room: open the bookshelf and let pupils choose. “One page at a time” shows one page large on screen.
  • Read to me: where the device has a built-in voice, the story can be read aloud at a slower speed.
  • Poor internet: open a book once, then choose “Save this book for offline” on that device.
  • No devices: print the A4 PDF. Each book page gives the printed page count.
  • Whole class: show a story on a projector and read it together.

Privacy for your pupils

Pupils do not sign in, and the story pages ask for no personal information. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits in aggregate. Read our privacy pages before sharing the site with your school if you need more detail.
