# Web-exclusive books: Reading Trail steps 7 and 8

Status: draft manuscripts, 6 November 2026. Not published. These continue the six Reading
Trail steps from Sentences & Spelling, which end at -ing, -ed and -er. Phonics-led books
lead the /read/ shelf, so the next books carry on that path. Both are web-only for now.

Reading Trail books are phonics-led, not fully decodable, so list the tricky words below on
each book page and read each book aloud once before publishing.

## Step 7: Nutmeg's Stormy Barn

**Phonics focus:** letters changed by r: ar, or, er, ir, ur.
**Target words:** barn, dark, star, farm, corn, storm, torn, her, bird, chirp, turn.
**Tricky words:** cloud, thunder, morning.
**Characters:** Nutmeg, and one small unnamed bird.

| Page | Text | Talk together | Art |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nutmeg has a farm with a red barn. Her corn is tall, and the first stars are out. | What can Nutmeg see on her farm? | Nutmeg at dusk beside tall corn and a red barn, first stars above |
| 2 | Dark clouds start to form. A storm is coming! Nutmeg must get her corn to the barn. | Why must Nutmeg hurry? | Nutmeg with a full basket of corn cobs, wind rising, dark clouds rolling in |
| 3 | A small bird chirps on the fence. Her nest is torn, and she cannot fly far. | What is wrong with the bird? | The small bird on a fence with a torn nest beside it, Nutmeg stopping to look |
| 4 | Nutmeg turns back. She lifts the bird and runs for the barn door. Thunder roars in the dark. | What does Nutmeg choose to do first? | Nutmeg running to the open barn door holding the bird, basket left behind, rain starting |
| 5 | Morning comes. Some of the corn is torn, but the bird chirps on the porch. Nutmeg smiles. A farm is more than corn. | What does Nutmeg mean? | Bright clear morning, bent corn, the bird singing on the porch rail, Nutmeg smiling |

**End questions**

1. Who did Nutmeg help? *A small bird.*
2. Which two letters make the sound in "corn"? *o and r.*

## Step 8: Panda-Cake's Puddle Party

**Phonics focus:** y at the end of a word; -le endings.
**Target words:** rainy, sunny, happy, funny, muddy, little, table, apple, puddle, middle, giggle.
**Tricky words:** window, picnic, Ted-E-Bear.
**Characters:** Panda-Cake, Ted-E-Bear, and one unnamed duckling.

| Page | Text | Talk together | Art |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | It is a rainy day, and Ted-E-Bear is not happy. His sunny picnic will have to wait. | Why is Ted sad? | Ted indoors at a rainy window, picnic basket on the floor beside him |
| 2 | Panda-Cake has a little idea. She sets a table by the window with an apple cake. | What does Panda-Cake do? | Panda-Cake placing an apple cake on a small table by the window |
| 3 | Ted looks out. There is a puddle in the middle of the path, and a duckling is paddling in it. | What does Ted see? | View through the window: path, large puddle, duckling paddling, Ted and Panda-Cake looking out |
| 4 | "That looks funny!" says Ted. They run out and jump in the puddle. Splash! They giggle. | How does Ted feel now? | Ted and Panda-Cake mid-jump in boots, water splashing, duckling watching happily |
| 5 | They come in muddy and happy, and share the apple cake. A rainy day can be a jolly day too. | What changed Ted's day? | Both muddy and smiling at the table, sharing cake, rain still falling outside |

**End questions**

1. What was in the middle of the path? *A puddle.*
2. Which word ends like "happy": funny or table? *Funny.*
