# Web-exclusive book: Rosa's Copán Report

Status: published 9 November 2026 at /read/rosas-copan-report/, before the Honduran
read-through, which is still wanted. Page text now lives in `data/web-books.json`. This is the older companion to
*Polly Roger and the Red Wings of Copán*. Rosa, an exchange student from Honduras, gives her
class a report on her home. It is written for readers aged about 9 to 12, including older
pupils learning English who need a grown-up topic in plainer language.

Needs the same Honduran read-through as the Polly book.

| 6 | Scarlet macaws almost disappeared from the valley. Then people began to build nests, watch the chicks and teach visitors. Now macaws fly over Copán again. **End questions**

1. What covers the steps of the Hieroglyphic Stairway? *More than a thousand carved glyphs.*
2. How did people help the macaws return? *They built nests, watched the chicks and taught visitors.*
3. Why are history and birds linked at Copán? *The Maya carved macaws and valued them, and macaws live in the valley today.*

**Word list for the book page:** exchange student, border, glyph, identity, protect.
