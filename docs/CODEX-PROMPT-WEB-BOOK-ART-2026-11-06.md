# Codex prompt: web book art (2026-11-06)

You are making illustrations for the SnackPack Universe free reading shelf. The monorepo
root is `C:\snackpack-universe`, and the website is the nested repo `website-snackpack-universe/`.

Read these in full before you paint anything:

1. `website-snackpack-universe/docs/ART-BRIEF-WEB-BOOKS-2026-11-06.md` (the rules and delivery paths)
2. `website-snackpack-universe/docs/BOOK-BRIEF-POLLY-ROGER-COPAN-2026-11-06.md`
3. `website-snackpack-universe/docs/BOOK-BRIEF-ROSAS-COPAN-REPORT-2026-11-06.md`
4. `website-snackpack-universe/docs/BOOK-BRIEF-READING-TRAIL-7-8-2026-11-06.md`

The job is 20 page pictures across four books, one character sheet, and a Big Two tile and
social card. Do the sets in order.

- Open each character's portrait and the style reference before painting, and use them as
  reference images. On-model characters are the main thing that has gone wrong before.
- Polly Roger is a red macaw and keeps her purple and gold coat and gold feathered hat on
  every page. Rosa is a scarlet macaw and an exchange student from Honduras, in a plain white
  collared shirt, blue skirt and small backpack, with no flag, badge or writing. Paint Rosa's
  sheet first and keep her identical on every page. Wild macaws wear no clothes.
- For Copán, look at reference photos of the real site and follow the rules in the brief.
  Show no people, no invented glyphs and no temples from other cultures.
- Make one proof for each book and compare it with the references, then paint the rest.
- No words, letters or digits in any book picture. Each picture is one full-bleed scene.
- Use new filenames in the delivery paths only. Do not overwrite existing files, and do not
  edit website pages, shelf data, app code, or anyone else's changes. Other sessions work in
  this checkout, so stage only your own new files and never reset, stash or discard anything.
- Do not publish, push or import anything into `read/`.

When you finish, write `docs/art-incoming/2026-11-06/DELIVERED.md` and commit only your new
files. Then report which files you delivered and which pictures you are least sure about.
