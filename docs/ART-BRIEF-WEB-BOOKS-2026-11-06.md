## House style

Match the current /read/ paintings: rich, warm, detailed storybook light with soft depth
and expressive animal characters. Style reference:
`read/polly-rogers-pirate-cove/pages/polly-roger-pirate-cove-p1.jpg`.

Every prompt ends with: **Full-bleed image only with absolutely no words, labels, arrows,
letters, digits, borders, logos, or watermarks.**

Each picture is one single scene, never split or stacked. Paint what the page text says,
so a child can point to it while the page is read aloud. No other SnackPack character

## Set 0: Rosa

Paint one sheet of Rosa on a plain background: front, side and flying. She is an accurate
scarlet macaw: red body, yellow and blue wing bands, pale bare face patch, pale upper beak.
She has a kind, lively expression, a little slimmer and more natural-looking than Polly.
Children must be able to tell the two apart at a glance. Then use this sheet for every page.

## Set 1: rules for Copán

Real places and a real culture are shown here, so accuracy matters more than drama.

- Use reference photos of the Copán Maya site in Honduras. Look at the Great Ballcourt:
  a long grassy court between sloping stone sides, with carved stone macaw heads.
- Use a green river valley and low hills, with tall trees around pale, weathered grey stone.
- Do not use stepped jungle pyramids from other sites, Aztec designs, gold idols, lava,
  skulls or anything frightening. No invented glyphs. Show carving as weathered, simple forms.
- No people on any page, and nobody in traditional dress.
- Page 5: a plain wooden nest box fixed high in a tree, with two chicks looking out.
- Page 6: a baleada is a thick, soft folded flour tortilla with refried beans. Show it on a
  plain plate on a low stone wall away from the carvings, never on the monument.
- Scarlet macaws in flight on pages 4 and 6 must look natural: red, yellow, blue, long tails.
- Never show a bird in a cage.

## Set 2 and Set 3 notes

- Nutmeg's bird is small, plain brown and unnamed. Keep the storm exciting, not scary.
- Panda-Cake's duckling is a plain yellow duckling. Rain should feel cosy.
