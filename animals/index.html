Animal field notes

Meet the animals

Explore the real animals that inspire SnackPack characters.

Blue jaysBlue jays eat acorns and may store some to eat later.Bottlenose dolphinsA bottlenose dolphin breathes through a blowhole on top of its head.Brown bearsBrown bears use strong claws to dig up food such as roots.CentipedesA centipede has one pair of legs on each segment that carries legs.ChickensChickens take dust baths to help keep clean.DogsDogs have a well-developed sense of smell. Some working dogs learn to pick out a particular scent.ElephantsAn elephant’s trunk joins its nose and upper lip. The trunk has no bones.Giant pandaA special wrist bone helps a giant panda grip bamboo as it eats.GiraffesGiraffes are the tallest animals that live on land.MiceHouse mice use their whiskers to feel the surfaces they explore.OctopusesAn octopus has eight arms. The arms carry suckers that help it grip.OwlsSpecial feather edges help many owls fly with very little sound.PenguinsA penguin pushes through the water with its flippers and steers with its feet and tail.Rabbits and haresRabbits and hares can turn their long ears in different directions to listen.SharksA shark’s skeleton is made of cartilage, the same kind of flexible material found in the tip of your nose.TigersEach tiger has its own stripe pattern.Tree squirrelsEastern gray squirrels bury food in little stores to eat through winter.TurtlesA turtle’s shell is part of its body. It is joined to its spine and ribs.
