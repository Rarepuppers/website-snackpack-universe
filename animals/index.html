Meet the animals

Explore the real animals that inspire SnackPack characters.

Giant pandaA special wrist bone helps a giant panda grip bamboo as it eats.Brown bearsBrown bears use strong claws to dig up food such as roots.Tree squirrelsEastern gray squirrels bury food in little stores to eat through winter.TurtlesA turtle’s shell is part of its body. It is joined to its spine and ribs.Rabbits and haresRabbits and hares can turn their long ears in different directions to listen.DogsDogs have a well-developed sense of smell. Some working dogs learn to pick out a particular scent.PenguinsA penguin pushes through the water with its flippers and steers with its feet and tail.ChickensChickens take dust baths to help keep clean.MiceHouse mice use their whiskers to feel the surfaces they explore.OwlsSpecial feather edges help many owls fly with very little sound.
