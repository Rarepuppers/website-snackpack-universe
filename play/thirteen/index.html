How to play Thirteen (Tiến Lên)

Thirteen is a Vietnamese card game for four players, also called Tiến Lên or Tien Len. Everyone gets 13 cards. The first player to empty their hand wins the deal. Whoever holds 3♠ opens and must include it, either alone or in a legal combination such as 3♠ and 3♦.

In Standard, the ranks go from 3 up through King, Ace and 2. For cards of the same rank, suits go from spades to clubs, diamonds and hearts. For example, 9♥ beats 9♦.

Open Learn below the table for six short lessons on the opening card, suit order, passing and chops. Try the six Challenges when you’re ready to play out a hand from a tricky position. In a regular solo deal, Show legal move selects a combination you can review before playing.

Combinations

  • Single — one card.
  • Pair — two cards of the same rank.
  • Triple — three of a kind.
  • Sequence — three or more cards in a run (e.g. 5-6-7). Twos can't be used in runs in Standard rules.
  • Double sequence — three or more consecutive pairs.
  • Four of a kind — four cards of the same rank.

On your turn, play a higher combination of the same shape and length, or pass. Passing puts you out until the next trick. Once everyone else has passed, the last player to play clears the table and leads any legal combination.

In Standard, a chop (also called a bomb) is a special reply. Four of a kind or at least three consecutive pairs can chop a single 2. A pair of 2s can be chopped with at least four consecutive pairs.

Three-Deal Series

Play three deals solo or at a friend table. With Deals Won, the first player out earns one point each deal and the most wins takes the series. With Cards Left, each card left in hand adds one penalty point and the lowest total wins. Players with equal best scores share the result.

How to win more often

  • Count the dangerous cards. In Standard there are only four 2s. Once two or three have gone, an Ace becomes a strong single.
  • Keep useful runs together. A 5–6–7–8 sheds four cards in one play. If you split it to beat a single, check what combinations you’ll have left.
  • Lead with a weak combination. When you start a trick, consider playing a low pair you might struggle to use as a reply.
  • Save a chop for a 2. Four 8s or a run of three pairs can take the lead from an opponent who plays 2♥.
  • Watch the card counts. When an opponent is down to one or two cards, lead pairs or runs they're unlikely to be able to match, not singles.

Worked example in Standard. An opponent leads 2♥. You can chop it with 6♠ 6♣, 7♦ 7♥ and 8♠ 8♣. If everyone else passes after your play, you lead the next trick.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thirteen free here?

Yes — it plays free in your browser with no ads, no sign-in, and nothing to install.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Choose Play with friends and share the invite link. Up to four people can join, with no sign-in, and the computer plays any empty seats. Tables have no names or chat, and playing with friends needs an internet connection.

Who do I play against?

Three computer players at the difficulty you choose. Easy plays its lowest legal cards and spends 2s early, Normal leads carefully but follows like Easy, and Sharp holds back its 2s and bombs. Each difficulty keeps its own win–loss record on this device. Whoever holds the 3 of spades opens.

Why is the 2 so strong?

In Standard, 2 is the highest rank and 2 of hearts is the strongest single. A higher-suit 2 can beat a lower-suit 2, and a legal chop can beat any single 2. In Classic, 2 is the lowest rank and Ace is the highest.

What is the Daily deal?

The Daily gives everyone the same Standard deal and Normal difficulty for the UTC date. Your first finish counts toward your streak, and later attempts are practice. Hints are disabled during the Daily.

How do I play Thirteen with a keyboard?

Press Tab to move between cards and controls. Enter or Space selects a card or activates a control.

Does my progress save?

Yes. A solo game in progress is saved in this browser, so you can pick it up on the same device.

Can I play offline?

Solo play works offline after the browser has cached the game. Friend tables need an internet connection. Thirteen is also in Snackpack Brain Games Vol 2 on Android.
