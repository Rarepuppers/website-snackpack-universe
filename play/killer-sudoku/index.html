How to play Killer Sudoku

Killer Sudoku keeps every rule of ordinary Sudoku — each row, column and 3×3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 once — and adds cages: dotted outlines around groups of squares, each with a small number in its corner. The digits in a cage must add up to that number, and a digit cannot repeat inside a cage. On the Hard puzzles no digits are given at all; the cage sums are the only clues.

Levels

  • Easy — 24 puzzles with a few digits given to start you off.
  • Medium — 20 puzzles with only four or five starting digits.
  • Hard — 24 puzzles with no starting digits: pure killer.
  • Daily — one puzzle a day from the whole collection, the same for everyone.

Every puzzle was checked by a solver to have exactly one solution, so each one can be finished by reasoning alone.

Techniques that help

  • The 45 rule. Every row, column and box adds up to 45. If the cages inside a box add up to 41 except for one square sticking out, that square is 4.
  • Unique sums. Some sums have only one set of digits: a two-square cage of 3 must be 1 and 2, of 17 must be 8 and 9; a three-square cage of 6 must be 1, 2 and 3.
  • Pencil marks. Turn on Notes to jot down the digits a square could be, and clear them as you rule them out.
