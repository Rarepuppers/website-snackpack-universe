Why a daily puzzle at all?

Because a score only means something if the board is the same. Every puzzle here is generated from the date, so the Sudoku you solve is the Sudoku everyone else solved today — and the FreeCell deal is drawn from a pool a solver has already proven winnable, which most daily card games can't promise.

Your streak and results are stored on your own device. There is no account, no email capture and no leaderboard server — nothing leaves your browser. Come back tomorrow and the set refreshes.
