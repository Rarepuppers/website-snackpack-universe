How it plays

Move with WASD or the left stick. Aim with the mouse or right stick. Roll to avoid danger, use your hero's ultimate, and draft weapons and upgrades as the waves grow tougher.

Last Bastion is free to play in your browser, with no download and no ads. Your progress is stored locally on this device; the game offers save export and import in Settings.
