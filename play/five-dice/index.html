How to play Dice Score

Dice Score is the classic roll-and-score dice game. Each turn you roll five dice, keep the ones you like, and roll the rest again — up to three rolls in all. Then you must score the dice in one empty box on your scorecard. There are thirteen boxes, so a game is thirteen turns, and every box is filled exactly once, even if that means scoring a zero. The skill is in the choices: which dice to hold, when to stop rolling, and which box to spend on a disappointing roll so the valuable ones stay open.

The scorecard

  • Ones to Sixes — the total of the dice showing that face. Reach 63 across these six and you earn a 35-point bonus: that is three of each face.
  • Three of a kind / Four of a kind — the total of all five dice, if at least three (or four) match.
  • Full house — three of one face and two of another, worth 25.
  • Small straight — four in a row (1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5 or 3-4-5-6), worth 30. Large straight — five in a row, worth 40.
  • Five of a kind — all five dice the same, worth 50. Every further five of a kind adds a 100-point bonus and can be scored in any open box at its full value.
  • Chance — the total of any roll. Your safety net.

Modes

  • Solo — chase your best score.
  • vs CPU — a relaxed computer opponent; Sharp CPU plays to protect its big boxes.
  • 2 players — pass the device between turns.
  • Daily — the same dice for everyone today, so scores are worth comparing.

Tips

  • Early on, aim for the upper bonus: three fives or three sixes in their own boxes is worth far more than it looks.
  • Holding four in a row with rolls left gives a fair chance at a large straight.
  • Save Chance for a bad roll late in the game rather than spending it early.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dice Score free?

Yes — it plays free in your browser with no ads, no sign-in, and nothing to install.

Do I have to use all three rolls?

No. Score as soon as you like; any roll can go in any open box.

What if nothing fits?

You still fill a box — pick one you were unlikely to score well anyway and it records a zero.

Does my game save?

Yes, on this device. Close the tab mid-game and you will be offered your game back next time.
