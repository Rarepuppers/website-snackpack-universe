How to play Target Shooting Arena

You get 12 shots. Tap or drag inside the goal to aim, set power, and shoot at the moving target. A normal target hit scores points. A bullseye scores more. Consecutive hits raise your combo multiplier.

Rookie mode gives larger, slower targets. Pro and Legend make targets smaller, faster, and less forgiving, so timing and power matter more.

The 12 shots are all you get per round, so treat power as the setting you commit to before you refine aim — not the other way around. A rushed high-power shot at a fast-moving target usually costs more than a calmer, lower-power hit that keeps your combo alive. Because a bullseye scores more than a normal hit but a miss breaks your streak entirely, the highest scores in Legend mode come from players who take the safe hit rather than force every shot into the centre ring.

Tips for a higher score

  • Lead the target slightly when it is moving fast.
  • Use moderate power unless you are aiming for high corners.
  • A regular hit keeps the combo alive, so do not force every bullseye.
  • Watch the ring size: smaller targets are worth more points.

Frequently asked questions

Is Target Shooting Arena free?

Yes. It plays free in your browser with no ads, no sign-in, and nothing to install.

Does Target Shooting Arena work on mobile?

Yes. Tap or drag on the goal to aim, then press Shoot.

How is the score calculated?

Each hit scores based on how close it lands to the centre of the moving target, with a bonus for a true bullseye. Consecutive hits raise a combo multiplier, so a run of regular hits can out-score a single lucky bullseye that breaks your streak.

What changes between Rookie, Pro and Legend?

The target itself: Rookie is larger and moves slowly, Pro shrinks it and speeds it up, and Legend makes it smallest and fastest, so higher difficulties reward precise leading and timing rather than raw power.

Is this an official World Cup game?

No. It is an original SnackPack Studios soccer game and is not affiliated with FIFA, the World Cup, any team, league, or governing body.
