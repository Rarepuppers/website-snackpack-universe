How to play Sokoban

Sokoban is a warehouse puzzle invented in Japan in 1981: the name means "warehouse keeper". You stand in a small storeroom full of crates, and every crate has to end up on one of the marked targets. You can push a crate by walking into it, but you can never pull one, and you can only push one crate at a time. That single rule is the whole game, and it is why a room that looks easy can trap you: push a crate into a corner and it can never come out again.

Move with the arrow keys, W A S D, a swipe on the board, or the arrow pad. Tap any floor square you can reach and the keeper walks there along the shortest path without pushing anything. Undo takes back one move at a time, and Restart puts the room back as it began.

The rooms

  • Starter Pack: 12 warm-up rooms that teach pushing, corners and walls.
  • Workshop Pack: 12 warehouse rooms with two and three crates.
  • Depot Pack: 8 step-up rooms between the beginner and expert rooms.
  • Delivery Trail Sampler: 2 relaxed rooms from a longer collection.
  • Freight Yard: 8 expert rooms with three crates.
  • Long Haul: 4 long plans of 40 moves and more.
  • Daily room: a new small room every day, the same for everyone, sized for a short break.

Every room was solved by a computer search that checks every possible plan, so each one has a solution and an exact par: the fewest moves that finish it. Matching par means you found a perfect solution. Your best for each room stays on this device.

Tips

  • Never push a crate into a corner that is not a target: nothing can move it again.
  • Crates against a wall can only slide along that wall, so check there is a target somewhere on it first.
  • Plan the last crate first. Work out which crate goes on the hardest target, and keep a path open to it.
  • Count your pushes. Par counts every step, so a short walk around a crate can beat a long detour.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sokoban free here?

Yes. All 46 rooms and the daily room play free in your browser with no ads, no sign-in, and nothing to install.

What is the daily room?

One new room each day, the same for everyone, sized for a two-to-five-minute break. Today's and yesterday's rooms play here; every past daily, plus 116 rooms in all, is in Snackpack Brain Games 3 on Android.

What does par mean?

Par is the fewest moves that solve the room, found by a solver that checks every possible plan.

I'm stuck. Can the room still be solved?

Every room has a solution, but a crate in a corner or two crates side by side against a wall can make it impossible from where you are. Undo until the crates can move again, or press Restart.
