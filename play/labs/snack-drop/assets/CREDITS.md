# Snack Drop asset credits

The D1 models, icons, and sounds were made for SnackPack Studios. The Corgi
model follows the SnackPack character canon and the existing Corgi art.
D1 uses no third-party art or recordings.

The D1 model revisions, D2 models, and picker icons were made for SnackPack
Studios. The picker icons were rendered from the exported game models.
D2 uses no third-party art or recordings.

D3–D6 tool and menu icons, effects, models, puppets and original sounds
were made for SnackPack Studios. The character models follow the shared
SnackPack canon. D4 and D5 also reuse four unmodified SnackPack Studios
files from Snack Haul.
