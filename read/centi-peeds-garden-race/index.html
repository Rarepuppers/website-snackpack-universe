Centi‑Peed’s
Garden Race

A four-page garden race about stopping to help a friend when a ribbon is within reach.

Read togetherKindness4 pages
Begin the story

5 printed pages · 0.9 MB · A4 or US Letter. Download PDF · Open PDF to printOpens in a new tab. Use the PDF viewer's Print control. To use less ink, read online and choose only the questions on page 5 in Print.

Page 1 of 4

Centi-Peed laces his many racing boots and announces he will win by one hundred steps. Shelly-Pop quietly wishes everyone luck.

Talk together: How is Centi-Peed feeling before the race?
Page 2 of 4

Wise Owl claps the starting signal. Centi-Peed springs ahead so quickly that his bootlaces hum.

Talk together: What begins the race?
Page 3 of 4

He zooms past the roses, then hears Shelly call: a fallen twig has pinned her race number. Centi skids to a stop.

Talk together: Why does Centi-Peed stop while leading?
Page 4 of 4

After he frees Shelly, they cross the finish together behind the others. Centi wins no ribbon, but all his boots join Shelly’s happiest dance.

Talk together: What matters more than the ribbon at the finish?
The end · Great reading

Why did Centi-Peed stop during the race?
