Nutmeg’s
Garden Morning

A four-page garden story about a seed, a lot of waiting and growth you cannot see.

Read togetherNature4 pages

Page 1 of 4

Nutmeg presses a seed into the garden bed. She checks before lunch. Nothing. She checks after lunch. Still nothing. Perhaps she should dig it up and see what is wrong.

Talk together: Why does Nutmeg want to dig up the seed?
Page 2 of 4

Hen-Berry pats the soil back into place. “Give it a little water,” she says. “Then leave it tucked in.” Nutmeg puts down her trowel. Waiting feels harder than digging.

Talk together: Why might leaving the seed alone help it?
Page 3 of 4

Each morning Nutmeg checks the soil and waters when it needs a drink. On the seventh morning, she spots a green sprout lifting a crumb of earth. “You were there all along!”

Talk together: What did Nutmeg do while she waited for the sprout?
Page 4 of 4

That evening Nutmeg sits beside her little garden. The sprout is hardly taller than a pebble, but she can find it at once. Tomorrow she will bring water, not her trowel.

Talk together: What does Nutmeg plan to do differently next time?
The end · Great reading

Why will Nutmeg bring water rather than dig up the seed next time?

Show the answer

She has seen that a seed can grow while it is hidden. The sprout appeared after she cared for the soil and gave it time.

Why did Hen-Berry cover the soil again?

Show the answer

To leave the seed tucked in while it grew.
