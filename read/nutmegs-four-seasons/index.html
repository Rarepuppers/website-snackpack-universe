Nutmeg’s
Four Seasons

A four-page story that follows one little oak tree through spring, summer, autumn and winter.

Read togetherNature4 pages
Begin the story
Page 1 of 4

In spring, Nutmeg plants one acorn beneath the old oak blossom. She promises the bare patch that something small can become something strong.

Talk together: What promise does Nutmeg make in spring?
Page 2 of 4

Summer arrives hot and dry. While the Baby Bulldogs splash, they carry one little bucket each to Nutmeg’s drooping shoot.

Talk together: How do the Bulldogs help during summer?
Page 3 of 4

In autumn, Nutmeg gathers acorns, but leaves the finest one beneath her young tree. A hungry jay needs winter food too.

Talk together: Why does Nutmeg leave one acorn behind?
Page 4 of 4

Frost silvers the hill, and the little tree stands firm. Nutmeg cannot hurry a whole year, but she can care for what grows through it.

Talk together: What has Nutmeg learned by winter?
