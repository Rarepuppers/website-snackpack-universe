Ted‑E‑Bear’s
Short‑Vowel Kit

A five-page den-building story for practising short-vowel words like box, rug, map, peg and cup.

Beginning readerShort vowelsRead together
Begin the story
Page 1 of 5

Ted has a big box, a red rug, and a map. His den is by his bed.

Talk together: Where is the den?
Page 2 of 5

The box is on top of the rug. The map is in a gap. Oh no!

Talk together: Where is the map?
Page 3 of 5

A cup and a peg are in his bag. Ted can put the cup by the box. It can sit up!

Talk together: How does the cup help?
Page 4 of 5

Ted has a peg. The peg can pin the map to the den. Ted can sit on the rug.

Talk together: What can pin the map to the den?
Page 5 of 5

Ted is in his den. His kit did a big job. It is fun to sit on the red rug!

Talk together: Why is Ted proud of his kit?
The end · Great reading

What does Ted put by the box?

Show the answer

A cup. On page 3, Ted takes a cup from his bag and puts it by the box.
