Polly Roger’s
Pirate Cove

A four-page pirate adventure about buried gold and a lighthouse that guides everyone home.

Page 1 of 4

From the mast, Polly Roger spots a lighthouse with a broken lamp. Without it, small boats cannot find the cove after dark.

Talk together: What problem does Polly see from the mast?
Page 2 of 4

Captain Beakon’s treasure map promises gold beneath a crooked palm. Polly imagines a mountain of crackers all for herself.

Talk together: What does Polly first want to buy with the treasure?
Page 3 of 4

They reach the rocky shore and uncover the X as the sun begins to sink. The dark lighthouse waits across the bay.

Talk together: Why must the crew decide quickly?
Page 4 of 4

The chest is full of gold coins. Polly keeps one shiny coin for the ship and spends the rest repairing the light that guides everyone home.

Talk together: What makes Polly’s treasure choice generous?
