Hen‑Berry’s
Helping Day

A four-page story about spilled berries and the difference between giving advice and really helping.

Read togetherKindness4 pages
Begin the story
Page 1 of 4

Hen-Berry carries her basket towards the picnic. Snap! The handle breaks. Berries tumble across the path. She scoops them into her wings, but each time she bends down, another berry rolls away.

Talk together: Why is gathering the berries difficult for Hen-Berry?
Page 2 of 4

Ted-E-Bear wants to share an idea. “You need a basket with a stronger handle.” Hen-Berry watches a berry roll past his foot. “Could you pick that up first?” she asks.

Talk together: Why does Hen-Berry ask Ted to pick up the berry first?
Page 3 of 4

Wise Owl stops beside them. “What would help?” Hen-Berry shows her full wings. Wise Owl listens, and Ted brings a bowl. At last Hen-Berry can put the berries down.

Talk together: How does asking and listening help the friends choose what to do?
Page 4 of 4

They set off with a small bowl each. Hen-Berry can walk without dropping anything. “Next time,” says Ted, “I will ask first.” Hen-Berry smiles. “You can help carry these now.”

Talk together: What has Ted learned about helping a friend?
The end · Great reading

Why was bringing a bowl more useful than giving basket advice?

Show the answer

It let Hen-Berry put down the berries she was holding. Listening helped Ted solve the problem Hen-Berry had right then.
