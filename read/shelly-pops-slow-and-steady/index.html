Shelly‑Pop’s
Slow and Steady

A four-page picnic story about noticing a quiet shortcut before the rain arrives.

Read togetherAdventure4 pages
Begin the story

5 printed pages · 1.0 MB · A4 or US Letter. Download PDF · Open PDF to printOpens in a new tab. Use the PDF viewer's Print control. To use less ink, read online and choose only the questions on page 5 in Print.

Page 1 of 4

Shelly-Pop stretches at sunrise before carrying berry cakes to the hilltop picnic. Grey clouds tell her she cannot waste the morning.

Talk together: Why does Shelly-Pop begin early?
Page 2 of 4

The other walkers hurry down the wide path, but Shelly stays calm enough to notice ants marching through a crack in the old wall.

Talk together: What does Shelly notice because she stays calm?
Page 3 of 4

Beside the ants, a line of white pebbles marks a safe shortcut through the clover. Shelly follows it without tipping one cake.

Talk together: How do the pebbles help Shelly?
Page 4 of 4

Shelly arrives first and covers the picnic just as rain begins. Slow did not mean late; careful helped her see what rushing feet had missed.

Talk together: Why does Shelly reach the picnic first?
