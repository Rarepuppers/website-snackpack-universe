Foxy‑Lox’s
Mystery Trail

A four-page mystery: a forgotten picnic basket, a trail of crumbs and a kind explanation.

Read togetherMystery4 pages
Begin the story
Page 1 of 4

Foxy-Lox finds a small hoof print beside a picnic basket that no one has collected. A storm will soak everything by evening.

Talk together: Why must Foxy-Lox solve the clue soon?
Page 2 of 4

A winding trail of apple crumbs leads downhill, but halfway along, the crumbs divide. Foxy checks which path has fresh bent grass.

Talk together: How does Foxy choose the correct trail?
Page 3 of 4

Behind a mossy log lies Ginger-Deer’s helper badge. Foxy remembers seeing the same badge pinned beside the town garden.

Talk together: What does the badge tell Foxy?
Page 4 of 4

The mystery ends at the garden, where Ginger is planting in the rain. She forgot the picnic while helping seedlings, so Foxy spreads the blanket above them both.

Talk together: What kind act explains Ginger’s disappearance?
The end · Great reading

Where did the clues lead Foxy-Lox?
