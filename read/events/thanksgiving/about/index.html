About Thanksgiving

Bark-B-Q has two Thanksgiving dates on his timetable. Both are right.

How it began · Try the question

Bark-B-QOne friend’s Thanksgiving is in October and another’s is in November. Which date goes on my timetable?

BooklavaBoth. Canada and the United States mark Thanksgiving on different dates, and people tell different stories about it.

When is it?

Thanksgiving in Canada is on the second Monday in October. In the United States it is on the fourth Thursday in November.

Who observes it?

Many families in Canada and the United States gather for Thanksgiving. Others spend the day in their own way or do not observe it. Some Indigenous people in the United States mark it as a National Day of Mourning.

Why does it matter?

Some families use the day to share a meal or name things they are grateful for. For others it is a time to remember what colonisation has meant for Indigenous people and to learn from their perspectives.

How it began

A harvest gathering at Plymouth in 1621 involved Wampanoag people and English colonists. It was later retold as a national origin story. Wampanoag histories also describe what happened after that gathering, including the loss of land and violence that followed.

Fun fact

One name, two dates

The Canadian and US dates use rules about weekdays. That is why each can fall on a different numbered day from one year to the next.

Try it

Which date belongs on Bark-B-Q’s timetable for Canadian Thanksgiving?

The second Monday in October.

Yes. The November date is for Thanksgiving in the United States.

The fourth Thursday in November.

That is the US date. Canada’s is on the second Monday in October.
