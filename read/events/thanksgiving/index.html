Read them in order

Each story happens a little later in the season than the one before. Every book stands on its own, so start anywhere.

  1. 1 · The week before

    Everybody Brought Potatoes

    Ages 7–9 · 26 pages · PDF

    Bark-B-Q is hosting Friendsgiving, and Eggs-Benedict has made a sign-up sheet so that nobody brings the same thing. Then Belgian-Waffle drops the sheet in the duck pond. He only read one word on it, so that’s the word he tells everybody.

  2. 2 · Thanksgiving Day

    Corgi-on-the-Cob and the Harvest Table

    Ages 5–7 · 24 pages · PDF

    Nutmeg has asked each friend to bring one thing to the Thanksgiving table. Corgi-on-the-Cob brings the corn, of course: six cobs for six friends. But the path to the barn has a stream, a hill and a very big pile of leaves.
