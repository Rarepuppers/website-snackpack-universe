About Diwali and Deepavali

A row of small lamps sparks a big question for Panda-Cake.

How it began · Try the question

Panda-CakeI’ve seen little clay lamps in pictures of Diwali. Do they tell the same story in every home?

BooklavaFamilies and communities can light lamps for different reasons. Let’s listen to a few of those meanings.

When is it?

Diwali or Deepavali usually falls in October or November on the Gregorian calendar. The timing and length of celebrations vary by calendar and community.

Who observes it?

Hindus in South Asia and around the world celebrate Diwali in many ways. Jains also observe Diwali with their own meaning. Many Sikhs mark Bandi Chhor Divas around the same time.

Why does it matter?

In one Hindu tradition, families remember Rama’s return to Ayodhya and welcome him with lights. For Jains, Diwali recalls Mahavira’s liberation. Sikhs describe Bandi Chhor Divas through Guru Hargobind’s release and the freedom he helped secure for others.

How it began

Stories, local practices and family memories all shape how people observe these occasions. Some households light diyas, make rangoli patterns or share sweets. A particular practice or explanation belongs to the family or tradition that shares it.

Fun fact

A tiny lamp

A diya is a small oil lamp, often made of clay. You may see rows of them near a doorway.
