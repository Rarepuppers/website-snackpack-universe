About Christmas

Why can Christmas be a snowy day in one story and a picnic day in another?

How it began · Try the question

BooklavaIn Australia and New Zealand, December is in summer. Let’s find out what people are celebrating, wherever they spend the day.

When is it?

Many churches celebrate Christmas on 25 December. Some churches use a different calendar and mark it on another date. The season outside depends on where you live.

Who observes it?

Christians celebrate Christmas around the world. Many other families join in with community gatherings, decorations or gift-giving. Each family decides how to spend the day.

Why does it matter?

Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus. Christians tell the Nativity story, in which Mary gives birth to Jesus in Bethlehem. Some families tell it with a crib scene, a church service or a song.

How it began

Over time, people added their own customs to the celebration. A family might sing carols in the evening, share a special meal, or hang decorations. The seaside lunch in Bark-B-Q’s story is one fictional family-and-friends Christmas gathering.

Fun fact

One date, two seasons

When Australia and New Zealand are in summer, many places north of the equator are in winter. Both can have Christmas in December.
