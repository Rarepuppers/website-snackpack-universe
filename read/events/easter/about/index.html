About Easter

Why does Easter move on the calendar, and why are the leaves turning in Nutmeg’s garden?

How it began · Try the question

NutmegMy cousin sent a picture of spring flowers. I’m sweeping autumn leaves. Can it be Easter in both gardens?

BooklavaIt can. Easter moves from year to year, and the seasons depend on where you live.

When is it?

Easter Sunday moves each year. In many Western churches, its date follows a church calendar rule based on spring in the Northern Hemisphere and a full moon. Some churches use a different calendar and may celebrate on a different Sunday.

Who observes it?

Christians around the world celebrate Easter. Customs vary between churches and families. Some people also join in activities such as egg hunts and family meals.

Why does it matter?

Christians celebrate their belief that Jesus rose from the dead. Holy Week is the time leading up to Easter. On Good Friday, Christians remember Jesus’s death. On Easter Sunday, they celebrate the resurrection.

How it began

Christians have marked Easter since the early centuries of Christianity. Later customs have taken many forms. Some churches light an Easter candle. Families in many places decorate eggs or share special foods.

Fun fact

Autumn Easter

Easter is in autumn in Australia and New Zealand. An egg hunt among fallen leaves makes just as much sense as one among spring flowers.
