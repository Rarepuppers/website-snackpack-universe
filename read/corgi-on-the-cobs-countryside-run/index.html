Corgi‑on‑the‑Cob’s
Countryside Run

A four-page countryside race about a caught ribbon and a friend worth turning back for.

Read togetherKindness4 pages
Begin the story

5 printed pages · 0.9 MB · A4 or US Letter. Download PDF · Open PDF to printOpens in a new tab. Use the PDF viewer's Print control. To use less ink, read online and choose only the questions on page 5 in Print.

Page 1 of 4

Corgi-on-the-Cob bounds through the farm gate when Ginger-Deer challenges him to race around the old mill. Corgi wants to win for once.

Talk together: What does Corgi hope will happen in the race?
Page 2 of 4

Corgi leaps the low fence and takes the lead. Behind him comes a snap: Ginger’s race ribbon has caught on a splinter.

Talk together: What happens after Corgi takes the lead?
Page 3 of 4

He turns back, nibbles the ribbon free, and runs alongside Ginger. Neither friend asks who is ahead now.

Talk together: Why does Corgi turn back?
Page 4 of 4

They cross the gate together and Corgi flops onto the mat laughing. He did not win the race, but he kept the friend he wanted to race again.

Talk together: What does Corgi value more than winning?
The end · Great reading

Why did Corgi-on-the-Cob give up his lead?

Show the answer

To free Ginger-Deer’s caught ribbon.

How did the friends finish the race?

Show the answer

They crossed together.
