Stripe‑Scout’s
Trail Clues

A four-page trail adventure about a map, a clue and choosing the path that leaves no friend behind.

Read togetherAdventure4 pages
Begin the story
Page 1 of 4

Stripe-Scout unrolls her map beside a fork in the trail. One route is short and muddy; the other is long and marked with tiny stars.

Talk together: What choice does the map give Stripe-Scout?
Page 2 of 4

Snack-Spector spots a crumbly clue beneath the star sign: “Choose the path that leaves no friend behind.” The short route suddenly looks less clever.

Talk together: What does the clue tell them to value?
Page 3 of 4

They take the longer path and help each other cross a wobbly bridge. When one board squeaks, Stripe-Scout waits until every paw is safely over.

Talk together: How does Stripe-Scout make the path safer?
Page 4 of 4

The trail ends at a picnic beneath a brilliant rainbow. The treasure is not just the food—it is arriving together with nobody missing.

Talk together: What is the real treasure in this story?
The end · Great reading

Why did Stripe-Scout choose the longer path?

Show the answer

She wanted everyone to arrive safely together. The clue mattered more to her than taking a shortcut.
