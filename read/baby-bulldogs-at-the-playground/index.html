Baby Bulldogs at the Playground

A four-page playground story about a shy puppy and a welcome that needs no words.

Read togetherFriendship4 pages
Begin the story
Page 1 of 4

The Baby Bulldogs chase their red ball across the grass until it rolls beside a puppy sitting alone. The puppy nudges it back without looking up.

Talk together: What might the quiet puppy be feeling?
Page 2 of 4

One Bulldog invites the puppy to the swing. Another talks so excitedly that the invitation gets lost beneath a tumble of words.

Talk together: Why might the invitation feel difficult to answer?
Page 3 of 4

Luckybug draws a chalk line with a star at each end. The puppy can join by rolling the ball across—no talking needed.

Talk together: How does the chalk game make joining easier?
Page 4 of 4

Soon the playground has one longer team and one very waggy new friend. The Bulldogs learn that a welcome can be an action, not only a question.

Talk together: What welcoming action worked best?
The end · Great reading

Why did the chalk game help the quiet puppy join in?

Show the answer

The puppy could play without having to speak. Rolling the ball let the puppy answer the welcome with an action.
