Captain Beakon’s
Star Watch

A four-page night-sky story about clouds, patience and spotting a planet beside the moon.

Read togetherNight sky4 pages

Page 1 of 4

Captain Beakon carries his telescope up the hill. Tonight he hopes to show Luckybug a planet. He looks up. Clouds stretch from one end of the sky to the other.

Talk together: What might Captain Beakon be thinking when he sees the clouds?
Page 2 of 4

A single star peeps through, then vanishes. “Shall we go home?” asks Luckybug. Captain Beakon sets up the telescope. “Five more minutes,” he says. “Let us see what happens.”

Talk together: Why might five more minutes make a difference?
Page 3 of 4

The clouds pull apart. Beside the moon, a bright planet shines through the gap. Captain Beakon looks, then steps aside. “Your turn.” Luckybug hurries to the telescope before the clouds return.

Talk together: Why does Captain Beakon give Luckybug a turn straight away?
Page 4 of 4

Soon the gap closes. They sit in the quiet and watch the sky. “I nearly missed it,” says Luckybug. On the way home, she keeps looking up, hoping for another gap.

Talk together: What shows that Luckybug feels differently about waiting now?
The end · Great reading

What would Luckybug have missed if she had gone home at once?

Show the answer

The chance to see the planet when a gap opened. Waiting gave the sky time to change, even though the gap did not last.

What glowed beside the moon?

Show the answer

A bright planet.
