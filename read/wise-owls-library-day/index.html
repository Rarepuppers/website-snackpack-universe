Wise Owl’s
Library Day

A four-page library story about a windblown poem and choosing when to share your work.

Read togetherKindness4 pages
Begin the story

Page 1 of 4

Wise Owl opens the library and finds Quill-Berry waiting outside with a folded page. “I wrote something,” Quill whispers, “but I am not sure I can share it.”

Talk together: Why is Quill-Berry waiting at the library?
Page 2 of 4

A gust slips through the door and carries the poem beneath a table. Quill-Berry freezes, certain everyone will laugh if they read it.

Talk together: How does Quill-Berry feel when the poem blows away?
Page 3 of 4

Meow-inara finds the page but does not read it. She returns it in the quiet corner and asks, “Would you like one friendly listener?”

Talk together: How does Meow-inara respect Quill-Berry?
Page 4 of 4

Quill-Berry reads her poem to two friends, then four, then the whole afternoon circle. Wise Owl adds a blank page to the display for whatever Quill writes next.

Talk together: What changed between the first and final page?
The end · Great reading

Why did returning the poem unread help Quill-Berry?

Show the answer

It let Quill choose when and with whom to share. Meow-inara respected Quill and offered one friendly listener.

What carried the poem beneath the table?

Show the answer

A gust of wind.
