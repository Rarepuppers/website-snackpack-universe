Luckybug’s
Art Gallery

A four-page gallery story about an unexpected splash, a new idea and a painting made together.

Read togetherCreativity4 pages

Page 1 of 4

Luckybug mixes a sunset orange on her palette for the gallery’s empty centre wall. She wants her first large painting to be perfect.

Talk together: Why is Luckybug taking such care with her palette?
Page 2 of 4

Quill-Berry carries over Ted’s portrait—and bumps the table. A blue splash lands across Luckybug’s glowing sky.

Talk together: What accident changes the painting?
Page 3 of 4

Luckybug nearly hides the canvas. Then Flutter-Butter says the splash looks like a river, so all three turn the smudge into a moonlit town.

Talk together: How do the friends transform the mistake?
Page 4 of 4

At the gallery opening, visitors point first to the blue river. Luckybug signs all three names because the accident became their shared idea.

Talk together: Why are three names signed on the artwork?
