The Cat and the Rat

A 8-page Stage 2 sound practice story. Prepare the declared exceptions together before your reader tries the words.

Stage 2DecodableRead together

Before reading together

This book has no added word endings or consonant blends. Practise the Stage 2 sounds and prepare a, is and the together. The other words use taught sounds, including short words such as in, at and and. Let your child try them, with help when needed. If your child stalls, cover the word and reveal it one letter at a time rather than saying it for them. Notice the story begins and ends in the same hat — ask what changed in between.

Prepare tricky words: a, is, the. Words needing an extra skill: none.
