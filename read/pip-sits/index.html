Pip Sits

A 6-page Stage 1 sound practice story. Prepare the declared exceptions together before your reader tries the words.

First readerLightly decodableRead together

Before reading together

This is a lightly decodable bridge for Stage 1. Most words use s, a, t, p, i, and n. The words a, is, and the need adult help, and the endings in sits, taps, tips, and naps may need a quick reminder.

Prepare tricky words: a, is, the. Words needing an extra skill: sits, taps, tips, naps.

Begin the story

8 printed pages · 1.8 MB · A4 or US Letter. Download PDF · Open PDF to printOpens in a new tab. Use the PDF viewer's Print control. To use less ink, read online and choose only the questions on page 8 in Print.

Page 1 of 6

Pip sits.

Sound it out: Point to each word, then read the whole sentence again.
Page 2 of 6

Pip is in a pan.

Grown-up help: “Is” and “a” are tricky words. Read them together if needed.
Page 3 of 6

Pip taps a tin.

Talk together: What does Pip tap?
Page 4 of 6

Pip tips the tin.

Grown-up help: “The” is a tricky word. What changed from the last page?
Page 5 of 6

Tap, tap, tap!

Join in: Tap three times as you read the words.
Page 6 of 6

Pip naps.

Talk together: Which three-letter words can you find in the story?
