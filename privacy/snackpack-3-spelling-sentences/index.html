Privacy policy

Sentences & Spelling

This Privacy Policy applies to SnackPack Sentences & Spelling, a mobile app published by SnackPack Studios. It explains what stays on the device, how the optional purchase works, and what choices parents have.

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Who this policy applies to

This policy covers Spelling & Sentence Games, also called SnackPack Sentences & Spelling, on Android and iPhone. The app is designed for children and families. It has no ads and requires no learning account.

Information stored on the device

The app stores the birth year entered at age setup, learning progress, reading positions, journal words, preferences, and saved library access on the device. The birth year helps the app choose age-appropriate settings. Learning and app activity records stay on the device. Reset controls in Settings let a parent clear learning records. Restoring a purchase does not restore these records.

Purchases and third-party services

Google Play processes the optional Full Library purchase on Android. Apple processes it through the App Store using StoreKit on iPhone. RevenueCat helps the app show the local price, check full-library access, and restore purchases. On Android, these checks can start when the app opens. On iPhone, the app starts with saved access and connects when a parent opens purchase options or restores a purchase, or when it checks a saved purchase or pending approval. RevenueCat receives a generated app user identifier, purchase and entitlement information where applicable, and technical information needed for the connection. The app sends no birth year, learning progress, or custom activity analytics to RevenueCat. SnackPack Studios does not receive payment card details.

Local reminders and narration

A parent may schedule an optional practice reminder on the device and turn it off in the app or device settings. Narration uses recordings bundled with the app or a device speech voice. A device speech voice may need to be downloaded in system settings.

Support and disclosure

When you contact support, SnackPack Studios receives your email address and the information you choose to send. Support messages may be kept for follow-up. SnackPack Studios does not sell personal information or use the app for cross-app tracking. Information may be disclosed when required by law.

Requesting data deletion

For help deleting a RevenueCat purchase record, email privacy@snackpackuniverse.com. We may ask for information to verify the request and locate the record. Apple and Google manage their own store records under their privacy policies. Uninstalling removes the app’s local learning data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@snackpackuniverse.com. For help with the app or a purchase, contact support@snackpackuniverse.com.
