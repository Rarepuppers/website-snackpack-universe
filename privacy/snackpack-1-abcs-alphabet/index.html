Privacy policy

ABC Read

This Privacy Policy applies to ABC Read (SnackPack ABC: Learn to Read), a learning app for young children published by SnackPack Studios. It explains what information the app handles, how that information is used, and what choices parents have.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to ABC Read on Android (Google Play package com.snackpackuniverse.abcalphabet). ABC Read is not yet available on other platforms. If a version for another platform is released, this policy will be reviewed and updated for it first.

Child-directed app status

ABC Read is designed for young children and family use and is treated as a child-directed app. SnackPack Studios does not use this app to sell personal information, show advertising, or require a child account.

Information stored on the device

  • Learning progress: the First Letters path, letters seen, finished books, stories and first readers, activity and quiz results, tracing progress, streaks, badges, reading time and the last page read.
  • Preferences: colour theme, sound and narration settings, favourites and the optional daily reminder setting.
  • Unlock status: whether the Full Library has been unlocked on this device.

This information stays on the device. It is not sent to SnackPack Studios or to any third party. Earlier versions asked for a birth year; the current version no longer asks for one and deletes any birth year an earlier version saved.

What the app does not do

  • No account: ABC Read does not need sign-in, account creation or a profile.
  • No ads: the app contains no advertising and blocks the advertising ID permission.
  • No analytics or tracking: the app includes no analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking SDK and sends no usage or learning data off the device.
  • No payment details: the optional one-time Full Library purchase is processed by Google Play. SnackPack Studios never receives or stores payment card details.

Notifications

A parent can turn on one optional daily reminder in the app's parent area. It is off by default and is scheduled as a local notification on the device; no notification service or server is involved.

Network access and off-device transmission

The app uses the network only for the optional Full Library purchase. RevenueCat, the purchase service the app uses, receives an anonymous App User ID and the purchase and entitlement information needed to sell, check and restore the purchase. The app sends RevenueCat no name, email address, age, book title, score, reading history or advertising identifier, and sets no custom attributes. Google Play processes the purchase and app distribution under its own policies. Read-aloud uses the speech voice installed on the device.

Sharing and disclosure

SnackPack Studios does not sell personal information. Only the purchase information described above is shared, with Google Play and RevenueCat, to operate the purchase. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Information on the device remains until it is reset in the app (Parents > Reset All Progress), cleared in device settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. Resetting progress keeps the Full Library unlocked. A parent may ask for the anonymous RevenueCat purchase record to be deleted by using Parents > Privacy or Data Request in the app, which prepares an email to privacy@snackpackuniverse.com with the anonymous ID needed to find it. Purchase records may be kept where the law or transaction integrity requires. Support emails may be kept for follow-up.

Security

SnackPack Studios uses reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app and keeps data collection to a minimum. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The version on this page is current, and the effective date changes when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about ABC Read, contact privacy@snackpackuniverse.com.
