Privacy policy

ABC Read

This Privacy Policy applies to ABC Read (SnackPack ABC: Learn to Read), a learning app for young children published by SnackPack Studios. It explains what information the app handles, how that information is used, and what choices parents have.

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Who this policy applies to

This policy covers ABC Read on Android and the iPhone and iPad edition when it becomes available. All editions use the identifier com.snackpackuniverse.abcalphabet.

Child-directed app status

ABC Read is designed for young children and family use and is treated as a child-directed app. SnackPack Studios does not use this app to sell personal information, show advertising, or require a child account.

Information stored on the device

  • Learning progress: the First Letters path, letters seen, finished books, stories and first readers, activity and quiz results, tracing progress, streaks, badges, reading time and the last page read.
  • Preferences: colour theme, sound and narration settings, favourites and the optional daily reminder setting.
  • Unlock status: whether the Full Library has been unlocked on this device.

This information stays on the device. It is not sent to SnackPack Studios or to any third party. Earlier versions asked for a birth year; the current version no longer asks for one and deletes any birth year an earlier version saved.

What the app does not do

  • No account: ABC Read does not need sign-in, account creation or a profile.
  • No ads: the app contains no advertising and blocks the advertising ID permission.
  • No analytics or tracking: the app includes no analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking SDK and sends no usage or learning data off the device.
  • No payment details: the optional one-time Full Library purchase is processed by Google Play on Android and by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad. SnackPack Studios never receives or stores payment card details.

Notifications

A parent can turn on one optional daily reminder in the app’s parent area. The device asks for notification permission when needed. Reminders are off by default and are scheduled on the device. You can turn them off in the app or in device settings.

Network access and off-device transmission

ABC Read uses RevenueCat to check prices and Full Library access and to process and restore the optional purchase with the device’s store. On Android these checks may run when the app starts or returns to the foreground, including if no purchase has been made. On iPhone and iPad the app starts with locally saved access. It connects to the purchase service after a parent opens the purchase screen, requests a restore or asks to include the purchase identifier in a privacy email. The purchase service can refresh access while it is active. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated App User ID, store transaction and entitlement information where applicable, and the technical information needed to handle the connection. The app supplies no name, email, age, book title, scores, reading history, advertising identifier or custom analytics attributes to RevenueCat. Google Play and the App Store process purchases and app downloads under their own privacy policies. Read-aloud uses device speech voices. A voice may need to be downloaded through device settings for offline narration.

Sharing and disclosure

SnackPack Studios does not sell personal information. The purchase service and the store for the device handle the purchase-related information described above. When you send a support or privacy email, SnackPack Studios receives your email address and what you choose to send. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Information on the device remains until it is reset in the app (Parents > Reset All Progress), cleared in device settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. Resetting progress keeps the Full Library unlocked. A parent may ask for the anonymous RevenueCat purchase record to be deleted by using Parents > Privacy or Data Request in the app, which prepares an email to privacy@snackpackuniverse.com with the anonymous ID if available. You choose whether to send it. Purchase records may be kept where the law or transaction integrity requires. Support emails may be kept for follow-up.

Security

SnackPack Studios uses reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app and keeps data collection to a minimum. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The version on this page is current, and the effective date changes when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about ABC Read, contact privacy@snackpackuniverse.com. For app or purchase help, contact support@snackpackuniverse.com.

This is the privacy policy for ABC Read on Google Play and for the App Store edition when it becomes available.
