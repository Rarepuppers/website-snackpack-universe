Privacy policy

SnackPack Arcade

This policy covers the free browser games at snackpackuniverse.com/play/. Last Bastion and Atlas Quest have their own policies.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

At a glance

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • The arcade shows no advertising.
  • You can invite friends to a Thirteen table with a link. Tables have no names and no chat.
  • Scores, settings and saved games stay in your browser.
  • We count how often each game is started and won, without identifying anyone.

Information stored in your browser

Best scores, win records, settings and games in progress are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them.

Game start and win counts

When you first play a game on a page, and when you win, your browser sends a fixed event name to GoatCounter, for example “thirteen start” or “thirteen win”. We use the totals to decide which games to improve. We add no account, identifier, score, typed text or cookie to these events.

Like any web request, each event reaches GoatCounter with ordinary request information such as an IP address and browser details. GoatCounter may use this to produce aggregate browser, device, language and location counts. Its own privacy policy applies to that processing. Story pages under /read/ do not send these events.

Friend tables

When you open a Thirteen table link, your browser connects to a server we run on Cloudflare. It holds the cards, whose turn it is and a random seat code, which is also stored in your browser so you can rejoin. There are no names and no chat. Tables are deleted after two idle hours. Like any web request, the connection includes ordinary request information such as an IP address, which we do not store with the table.

Page analytics and hosting

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate page visits and performance across this site. GitHub Pages hosts the site and handles ordinary web requests.

Children and families

Many arcade games suit children, and some suit adults better. We do not ask for a child’s personal information. If you believe we hold such information, email us and we will delete it.

Links to the app store and tips

Some pages link to Google Play or a tip page. Those services have their own privacy policies and only apply if you choose to open them.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if what the arcade sends changes. For questions, email support@snackpackuniverse.com.
