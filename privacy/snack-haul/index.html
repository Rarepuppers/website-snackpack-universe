Privacy policy

Snack Haul

This Privacy Policy applies to Snack Haul, a mobile game published by SnackPack Studios. In Snack Haul you drive a snack truck over hills and try to keep the load on the bed. This page explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Who this policy applies to

This policy covers Snack Haul on Android and the iPhone and iPad edition when it becomes available, the free browser version, and the optional website launch alert. Snack Haul is a general-audience game. It is not designed for or directed at children.

Information the app handles

  • Game progress and settings: Your coins, stars, medals, best times, trucks, upgrades, paint jobs, goals, the recorded pedal inputs of your best run on each route (used to draw your ghost), and your sound, music, vibration and graphics settings are stored on your device.
  • Purchase status: If you buy the optional one-time unlock, the app checks whether your account with the device’s app store owns it so the full game stays unlocked. See “In-app purchase” below.
  • Device-level technical information: Platform providers (Google Play, Apple, and RevenueCat) may process technical data such as device type, operating system version, IP address, or diagnostics when needed to operate their services.
  • Support contact information: If you email SnackPack Studios for support, the email address and information you include may be received and kept for support purposes.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account: Snack Haul does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • No advertising: The app shows no ads and does not use the device’s advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or tracking: The app sends no gameplay analytics and includes no analytics or advertising SDK. It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No microphone, location, contacts or photos: The app only plays sound. It does not request your microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Local storage

Snack Haul keeps all game progress on your device. In the mobile app, a second copy is kept in the app's own private storage so progress survives if the game view is reset. Neither copy is sent to SnackPack Studios. Your progress stays until you reset it in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Progress on the website version is stored in your browser and is separate from the app.

In-app purchase

Snack Haul is free to download and play, with an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the game. There are no subscriptions, no coin packs and no ads. Google Play Billing processes purchases on Android. Apple processes purchases through the App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad. SnackPack Studios never receives your payment card details. The app uses RevenueCat to check price and access, validate receipts, and restore the unlock. On Android the app may check access when it starts or returns to the foreground. On iPhone and iPad the app starts with the locally saved unlock status and connects to RevenueCat when you open the purchase screen or request a restore. The purchase service may refresh access while it is active. RevenueCat may process a randomly generated app user identifier, purchase and receipt information, and technical information needed to operate its service. The app sends no game progress to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy.

How information is used

  • To run the game: saving progress and settings on your device.
  • To deliver the unlock: checking and restoring the one-time purchase.
  • To fix problems: diagnosing crashes and bugs reported by platform providers.
  • To answer support requests: if you contact SnackPack Studios.

Children's privacy

Snack Haul is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no accounts, chat, ads or analytics, and keeps game progress on the device. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Website launch alerts

Adults can sign up for the Snack Haul launch announcement through Brevo. Brevo processes the email address to send a confirmation link and deliver the launch announcement after the address is confirmed. Use the unsubscribe link in the announcement or email privacy@snackpackuniverse.com to request removal.

Sharing and disclosure

SnackPack Studios does not sell personal information. The device’s app store and RevenueCat process information to distribute the app and operate the purchase. Brevo processes launch alert email addresses as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Retention and security

Information on your device stays until you reset it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Platform services use encrypted connections. RevenueCat purchase records may be kept as needed to restore purchases, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Support emails may be kept for follow-up and recordkeeping.

Your choices

  • Game data: Reset the game in Settings, or clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • RevenueCat data: Ask for deletion of the anonymous purchase record at privacy@snackpackuniverse.com.
  • Purchases: Manage purchases in the app store account you used to buy the unlock.

Requesting data deletion

Game progress lives on your device, so resetting the game, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes it. To ask for deletion of information held off the device, email privacy@snackpackuniverse.com with enough purchase details to find the anonymous Snack Haul record. The associated RevenueCat customer record, including the generated app user identifier, transaction information and technical identifiers, will be deleted within 30 days after verification. Google Play and Apple keep their own purchase records under their own policies. Deleting the RevenueCat record may affect restoring the purchase.

Step-by-step instructions: Snack Haul data deletion request.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The version on this page is the current one, and the effective date changes when material changes are made.

Contact

If you have privacy questions or support requests about Snack Haul, contact privacy@snackpackuniverse.com.
