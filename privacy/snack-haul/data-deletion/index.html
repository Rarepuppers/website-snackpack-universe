Data deletion

Snack Haul data deletion request

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with Snack Haul, a truck driving game published by SnackPack Studios. Snack Haul has no accounts, so there is no account to delete; game progress is stored on your own device.

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Delete your game progress

Coins, stars, trucks, upgrades, paint jobs, goals, ghost runs and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, choose Reset game in Snack Haul's Settings, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. On the website version, clear this site's data in your browser.

Request deletion of purchase data

  1. Email: Send a request to privacy@snackpackuniverse.com with the subject "Snack Haul data deletion request".
  2. Include purchase details: If you bought the unlock, include the approximate purchase date and the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt, if available. Do not send payment card details.
  3. Verification: SnackPack Studios may ask for enough information to match the request to the anonymous RevenueCat record for your purchase before deleting it.

Data deleted

After a verified request, SnackPack Studios will request deletion of the Snack Haul RevenueCat record associated with your purchase. This includes the randomly generated app user identifier, product identifiers, transaction information and entitlement state. Snack Haul sends no gameplay data, so there is no gameplay record to delete.

Verified RevenueCat deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days. Deleting the record may affect restoring the purchase later.

Data kept or controlled elsewhere

  • App store purchase records: Google Play and Apple keep purchase records under their own policies. SnackPack Studios cannot delete records held by either store.
  • Support emails: Emails you send to SnackPack Studios may be kept separately for support, fraud prevention, legal, tax, accounting or recordkeeping purposes.

Questions

For privacy questions about Snack Haul, contact privacy@snackpackuniverse.com. See also the Snack Haul privacy policy.
