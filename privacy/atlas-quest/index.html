Privacy policy

Atlas Quest

This policy explains how Atlas Quest handles information in its web and installable PWA release. Atlas Quest is a local-first, ad-free geography learning game published by SnackPack Studios.

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Current release at a glance

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, payment, chat or location permission is used.
  • No gameplay analytics are sent in the current release.
  • Scores, learning progress and settings remain in your browser unless you choose to export them.

Information stored on your device

Atlas Quest stores completed rounds, scores, country and topic mastery, spaced-review dates, Daily Challenge results, achievements, display and sound preferences, and install-prompt state in browser storage. This information supports progress, revision scheduling and accessibility preferences. It is not synced to a SnackPack account.

Information sent by Atlas Quest

Atlas Quest uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate page-view, referral and real-user performance measurement. Cloudflare's beacon does not receive gameplay events, learning progress, country or question history, answers, advertising identifiers or precise location from Atlas Quest. The separate gameplay analytics interface remains inert and does not transmit data.

Hosting, analytics and ordinary web requests

Loading the game or this policy requires ordinary web requests. Cloudflare provides hosting, security and privacy-focused Web Analytics, and may process connection information such as an IP address, browser details, referring page, request time and performance measurements. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage for its measurement. Atlas Quest does not combine this information with local learning history.

Exports, imports and sharing

Progress export creates a JSON backup on your device. Import reads the file you select and replaces local Atlas Quest progress only after confirmation. Result-card sharing uses the browser or operating-system share interface when you choose it. Atlas Quest does not learn the recipient or destination.

Support and feedback

The in-game contact controls open your email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send a message. If you contact us, your email provider transmits the address, message and any information you include. Support messages may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Do not include sensitive personal information or detailed information about a child.

Children and families

Atlas Quest contains child-appropriate geography content and may be used by school-age learners and adults. It does not currently create an age-dependent data path. If accounts, advertising, payments, social features or new data-processing services are proposed, the audience declaration and safeguards will be reviewed before release.

Your choices and deletion

  • Use Reset local progress in Atlas Quest to remove stored scores and learning progress.
  • Clear the site data in your browser to remove all Atlas Quest browser storage and caches.
  • Delete any exported backup or result image using your device’s file controls.
  • Choose whether to open an external policy or email contact link.

Data sources

Atlas Quest includes an offline Credits & data sources screen listing its reviewed geographic datasets, versions, retrieval and review dates, definitions and licence notices. Those source records describe educational content, not player information.

Security and changes

SnackPack Studios uses reasonable measures to protect the sites it operates and minimizes off-device data by keeping learning history local. No security method is absolute. If Atlas Quest’s data handling materially changes, this page and its effective date will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or feedback about Atlas Quest, email support@snackpackuniverse.com.
