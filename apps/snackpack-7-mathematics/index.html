Mathematics

Character-led, curriculum-aligned maths practice for ages 8-12 — fractions, decimals, geometry, statistics, stories, riddles and daily challenges, with adaptive difficulty and read-aloud support.

What's included

Thousands of practice questions across fractions, decimals, ratios, percentages, geometry and statistics, plus character-led story books, maths riddles, daily rotating challenges, adaptive difficulty, read-aloud support and a dyslexia-friendly font option. Some content is included with a one-time Pro unlock — no subscription.
