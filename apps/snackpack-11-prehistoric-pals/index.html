Questions

Common questions.

Is Prehistoric Pals free?

Yes — it's free to download, and the encyclopedia, fossil digs, diet lab, evidence board and era quizzes are all included. One optional one-time purchase unlocks the full 99-entry Amber Archive and the premium themes. No subscription, no ads.

Does it have ads?

No. There are no ads anywhere in the app and no third-party ad SDKs. The only purchase is the optional one-time premium unlock.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

Not at the moment. Prehistoric Pals is an Android app, available on Google Play only.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is built to work offline, so the encyclopedia, digs and quizzes keep working without a connection — useful for car journeys and flights.

How many dinosaurs are in the app?

The encyclopedia has 40 species covering dinosaurs and other ancient life, alongside 102 daily fossil facts and a 99-entry Amber Archive of deep-cut research. Periods run from the Triassic through the Jurassic and Cretaceous to the Paleogene.
