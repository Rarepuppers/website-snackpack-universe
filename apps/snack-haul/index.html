Drive it like it's fragile

Every load has its own physics.

The truck is only half the job. Crates slide, eggs crack, melons roll, jelly bounces and ice cream melts if you dawdle. Take a crest too fast and the load goes over the side; hit the depot wall and the medal goes with it. Stop inside the arrows with the load intact for three stars.

  • 8 kinds of cargo: crates, eggs, milk, jelly, melons, cakes, popcorn and ice cream.
  • Ramps, plank bridges, fords, ice, gusty wind, speed bumps and pickup stops along the way.
  • Upgrade the engine, suspension, tyres, cargo straps and fuel tank, five levels each.
  • Stars, bronze, silver and gold medals, and a challenge on every route.

Beat your ghost

Every route remembers your fastest run and replays it as a see-through truck beside you, labelled with its time. A live chip shows whether you are ahead or behind at the same point on the road, and the results tell you by how much you beat it. Until you beat him on a route, the rival driver Dash Dalloway races you instead.

A daily delivery and three daily contracts, a Weekly Cup of five routes, 30 Special Orders from six customers, Long Haul runs that go as far as your fuel allows, 39 goals, truck mastery, earned paint jobs, and a depot that grows from a shed and a dirt yard to neon lights and a café.

Frequently asked questions

Is Snack Haul out yet?

Not yet. Snack Haul is coming soon to Google Play for Android. Leave your email below and we'll tell you the day it lands.

Is Snack Haul free?

It's free to download. Bakery Hills, the daily delivery, the Weekly Cup, the Rusty Pickup and the Tart Tuk-Tuk are free. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other five regions and the rest of the trucks. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

What is the ghost?

Every route remembers your fastest run and replays it as a see-through ghost truck beside you. A live chip shows how far ahead or behind you are, and the results tell you by how much you beat it. Until you beat the rival on a route, Dash Dalloway races you instead. You can switch the ghost off in Settings.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed and the game plays offline, with your progress saved on your phone. The store is only contacted when you buy or restore the full-game unlock. See the Snack Haul privacy policy.
