Earth Science

A curriculum-aligned science app for students in Years 4-8, where quizzes, story books, riddles and mini-games all teach the same real science. Adaptive difficulty, two levels of hints, and an explanation worth reading behind every answer. No ads, no signup, no accounts.

Science learning

Learn the science, then play with it.

Earth Science is built around one idea: a student should meet the same concept several different ways. A quiz question, a story book, a riddle, a fact-check and a mini-game can all be about the rock cycle, and each one makes the next one land harder.

  • 841 science questions with an adaptive engine that follows each student's accuracy.
  • 94 story science books, written at the right reading level for each year group.
  • 116 Fact or Fiction claims, each with a detailed explanation of the verdict.
  • Branching choose-your-own-adventure stories built around real field-science safety decisions.
  • Local-first play with on-device progress, no ads, and no tracking SDKs.

Features

  • Adaptive science quiz: difficulty follows each student's accuracy, with two tiers of hints so nobody gets stuck.
  • Story science books: 94 curriculum-matched books with optional read-aloud narration.
  • Fact or Fiction: 116 surprising claims to judge, each with the reasoning behind the verdict.
  • Choose-your-own-adventure: branching stories where each choice teaches a real piece of field science.
  • Mini-games: Riddle Trail, Habitat Match, Weather Watch, Map Scavenger Hunt, Timeline Order, Food Chain Builder, Word Search, Would You Rather, Crossword, and pass-and-play Quiz Battle.
  • Explorer Journal: guided observation prompts that connect screen learning to the outdoors.
  • Streak calendar and achievements: a light habit tracker, and achievements that reward curiosity rather than only correct answers.
  • For grown-ups: a printable progress report, a parental gate on anything leaving the child-facing experience, and screen-reader labels throughout.
