Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about Badgify.

Is Badgify free?

Yes. Badgify is free to download, and free templates in every category are watermarked but usable right away. Pro is available as a $9.99 lifetime unlock, a $1.99 monthly or $6.99 annual subscription, or a $2.99 single watermark-free export.

What platforms is Badgify available on?

It's available now on Android via Google Play. An iOS version is planned for the future.

What kinds of certificates can I make?

157 templates across 11 categories: Gag & Meme, Workplace, Pets, Tech & Dev Culture, Gaming, Family & Friends, Adulting & Life Admin, Travel & Adventure, Academic & School, Sports & Race, and Holiday & Seasonal.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You can use the free templates, buy Lifetime Pro once, purchase one watermark-free export, or choose monthly or annual Pro. A subscription is optional.

Who makes Badgify?

Badgify is made by SnackPack Studios, part of the SnackPack Universe lineup of calm, family-friendly apps.
